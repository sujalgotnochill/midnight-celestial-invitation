MUSIC PLACEHOLDER INSTRUCTIONS

The `music.mp3` file in this project is intentionally empty and is not playable audio.
Replace it with a valid MP3 recording that you own or are licensed to use, preferably gentle instrumental piano with subtle sitar or ambient strings.
Keep the filename `music.mp3`, or edit `audio.source` in ../config.json.
Audio playback is user-initiated only; the site does not autoplay sound.
